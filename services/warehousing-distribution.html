Warehousing and cargo storage support in Egypt

AK INTERNATIONAL handles enquiries for commercial cargo storage and distribution associated with imports, exports and domestic delivery plans. Where a shipment needs temporary storage between arrival and onward movement, we can discuss handling and warehouse arrangements based on cargo, location and required duration.

Storage space and service scope must be confirmed for each request. The website does not represent that AK INTERNATIONAL owns warehouse facilities at every Egyptian port, or that storage is automatically available.

Storage planning, cargo handling and special requirements

Explain whether your cargo will arrive in containers, pallets, cartons or other packaging, and whether it needs unloading, pallet handling, short-term storage or a planned release schedule. Provide the expected cargo volume, gross weight, number of units and storage period to support a realistic enquiry.

Temperature control, restricted goods, bonded storage, dangerous goods and specialist handling require separate checks on facility suitability, permits and availability. These options should not be assumed to be included in a standard storage quotation.

Distribution, warehouse-to-door delivery and trucking

Storage is often one stage in a wider logistics movement. We can review requests involving pickup, onward trucking, staged dispatches or deliveries to factories, retailers and other commercial addresses. The routing and proposed schedule depend on cargo readiness, warehouse access and the destination requirements.

For related movements, see our land transport and container trucking services in Egypt and sea freight forwarding support. Each service and its costs are confirmed according to the requested scope.

Warehousing enquiries linked to Egyptian seaports

We welcome enquiries involving import and export cargo moving via Egyptian Mediterranean and Red Sea ports, including Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, East and West Port Said, Ain Sokhna, Suez, Adabiya, Safaga and Nuweiba, as well as other suitable Egyptian gateways and inland locations.

The list identifies potential shipment origins and destinations, not a guarantee of an AK INTERNATIONAL warehouse or storage contract at each port. Availability and permitted storage conditions must be reviewed case by case. You can also discuss connected customs clearance arrangements and import and export documentation.

Information needed for a warehousing and distribution enquiry

  • Preferred warehouse area or port, and the eventual delivery destination
  • Cargo description, packaging, unit count, gross weight and estimated volume
  • Arrival date, expected storage period and dispatch schedule
  • Loading, unloading, equipment or temperature-control requirements
  • Customs release status and any applicable permits or storage restrictions

Contact the AK INTERNATIONAL team with these details to discuss availability and an appropriate quotation. The main online freight form is intended primarily for transport enquiries; warehousing requirements can be sent through the contact channels.

Warehousing and distribution: frequently asked questions

Can I request warehouse support without booking sea freight?

Yes. You can submit a separate storage or distribution enquiry. The team will review the location, cargo and requested services before confirming what can be arranged.

Is bonded or temperature-controlled storage available for every shipment?

No such availability is guaranteed. These requirements depend on the facility, commodity, permissions and operating arrangements; please specify them before a quotation is prepared.

Can I enquire about cargo storage linked to ports outside Port Said?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for locations across Egypt, including Mediterranean and Red Sea gateways. Actual storage arrangements are assessed for each location and shipment.
