Sea Freight Services in Egypt

Request FCL, LCL, reefer and special-equipment ocean freight support for cargo moving to or from Egypt. Share your ports, cargo details and preferred timing for route-specific review.

Discuss your shipment →

FCL and LCL container shipping

Full container load (FCL) suits shipments requiring dedicated container space. Less than container load (LCL) supports smaller consignments sharing container capacity. Tell us your cargo dimensions and weight so the request can be reviewed against the route and equipment requirements.

Reefer and special equipment

For temperature-sensitive or oversized cargo, include the required temperature, dimensions, weight and handling instructions. Equipment availability and carrier acceptance must be confirmed for the specific shipment.

Sea freight through Egyptian ports

Plan shipments involving Egypt’s Mediterranean and Red Sea gateways. Example ports include Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, Port Said, East Port Said and El Arish on the Mediterranean side; and Ain Sokhna, Suez (Port Tawfik), Adabiya, Safaga and Nuweiba on the Red Sea side.

Other Egyptian seaports can also be specified in your enquiry. The suitable port, available carrier service, equipment and cargo acceptance depend on the route and shipment requirements; they must be confirmed before booking.

For related shipment arrangements, see customs clearance and land transport.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Origin country and seaport; destination country and seaport
  • Container size, type and quantity for FCL
  • Cargo description, gross weight and volume
  • Cargo readiness date and requested additional services
