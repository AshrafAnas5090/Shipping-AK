Air Freight Services in Egypt

AK INTERNATIONAL supports importers and exporters with international air freight enquiries to and from Egypt. We help coordinate cargo requirements for time-sensitive shipments, with routing, space and handling arrangements reviewed for each consignment.

Discuss your shipment →

International air freight for imports and exports

Air cargo can suit commercial shipments where delivery schedules, product requirements or supply-chain deadlines call for a faster mode of transport. AK INTERNATIONAL accepts enquiries for air freight movements into and out of Egypt, including general cargo and consignments needing a review of special handling requirements.

We assess each request against the proposed route, shipment details and available airline arrangements. A quotation request is not a confirmed flight booking or guarantee of transit time.

Air cargo gateways across Egypt

Enquiries may involve Cairo International Airport (CAI), Borg El Arab International Airport (HBE) serving the Alexandria region, or other Egyptian airports where the required cargo facilities and airline services are available. Airport choice depends on the goods, international connections and local handling requirements.

We review the available routing for each shipment; an airport named here does not imply that AK INTERNATIONAL maintains a physical office at that airport or that every cargo category is accepted there.

Air freight weight, dimensions and cargo handling

Air freight quotations need both gross weight and package dimensions, because chargeable weight may be based on the greater of actual and volumetric weight under the carrier’s applicable rules. Provide the number of pieces and the dimensions of each package to help avoid inaccurate estimates.

If cargo is fragile, perishable, temperature-sensitive, oversized or subject to restrictions, describe its condition and handling needs before shipment. Airline acceptance, permits and any special equipment must be confirmed for the specific goods and route.

Air freight documentation and customs coordination

For many commercial air shipments, the starting documents are a commercial invoice, packing list and air waybill details. Certificates of origin, permits, product approvals or additional customs documents may be required depending on the commodity and import or export procedure.

For connected services, discuss customs clearance in Egypt, onward land transport and import and export documentation support. The applicable requirements and service scope are verified during enquiry review.

What to include in an air freight quote request

  • Origin and destination country, airport or nearest city
  • Cargo description, package count and dimensions (length × width × height)
  • Gross weight, packaging type and shipment readiness date
  • Required delivery timing and contact details
  • Special handling, permits and customs documentation status, if applicable

Send these details through our freight quotation form so the team can review the appropriate options.

Air freight: frequently asked questions

Can I request air cargo shipping through airports outside Cairo?

Yes, you can submit an enquiry for other Egyptian airports. Our team will review the available airline route and whether suitable cargo facilities and handling arrangements exist for that shipment.

Does AK INTERNATIONAL publish instant air freight rates or guaranteed delivery times?

No. Rates, airline space and transit schedules are reviewed for each enquiry. The website does not issue instant airline prices or confirm air cargo bookings.
