Import and export logistics support in Egypt

AK INTERNATIONAL helps businesses coordinate freight movements into and out of Egypt. Whether you are importing commercial goods or arranging an export shipment, the first step is to confirm the cargo, trading parties, origin, destination and agreed Incoterms. We then review which parts of the movement require assistance: freight booking, documentation, customs coordination, inland transport or temporary storage.

Shipment arrangements and the scope of services are confirmed for each enquiry. This is a commercial logistics service, not a substitute for the importer's or exporter's own legal and regulatory obligations.

Import documentation and pre-shipment coordination

For imports into Egypt, the required documents may include a commercial invoice, packing list, transport document, certificate of origin and commodity-specific permits or certificates. Requirements depend on the goods, trading arrangement and applicable regulations; not every shipment needs the same documents.

Egypt's Nafeza national trade window supports customs-related electronic processes, including the Advance Cargo Information (ACI) / ACID process where applicable. It is important to check the current rules and complete any required steps before shipment departure. Our team can discuss documentation readiness and connected customs clearance support in Egypt; formal submissions and approvals remain subject to the responsible parties and authorities.

Export shipment preparation and paperwork

Export planning starts with the product description, destination-country requirements, consignee details, cargo readiness and shipping terms. Common paperwork may include an export invoice, packing list, transport document and origin or inspection certificates where required.

Exporter registration and inspection requirements must be checked against the relevant commodity and current rules. The General Organization for Export and Import Control (GOEIC) provides information on applicable registration and inspection services. We recommend confirming requirements before goods are packed or dispatched to the port.

Sea freight, air freight and inland delivery planning

Different cargo types require different transport arrangements. We can review requests involving FCL or LCL sea freight, reefer or special equipment, and air freight for time-sensitive commercial cargo. The most suitable option depends on cargo dimensions, weight, handling needs, route and target delivery date.

Where needed, shipments can also be discussed alongside port-to-door trucking and warehousing or distribution coordination. Costs, schedules, carrier services and any special approvals must be confirmed for the actual shipment.

Import and export enquiries across Egyptian ports

We accept enquiries concerning shipments through Egyptian Mediterranean and Red Sea gateways, including Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, East and West Port Said, Ain Sokhna, Suez, Adabiya, Safaga and Nuweiba, as well as other Egyptian ports where the requested service and cargo are operationally suitable.

Coverage is not limited to Port Said. Port names here describe potential trade routes and enquiry locations, not a claim that AK INTERNATIONAL operates a dedicated branch, customs office or warehouse at each location. Shipment feasibility and the relevant port procedures are reviewed case by case.

Details to send for an import or export quotation

  • Import or export direction, origin, destination and preferred Egyptian port or airport
  • Product description, HS code if known, cargo quantity, gross weight and packing dimensions
  • Shipment mode, FCL/LCL or container requirements, and estimated cargo-ready date
  • Incoterms, shipper and consignee details, and existing commercial or shipping documents
  • Services required: freight, customs coordination, inland delivery or warehousing

Use our freight quotation form for shipment routing and pricing enquiries, or contact AK INTERNATIONAL to discuss documentation and more complex import/export requirements.

Import and export logistics: frequently asked questions

Can freight, customs and inland delivery be coordinated together?

Yes. Describe the required services in one enquiry. The team will review the possible combined scope and confirm responsibilities before issuing a quotation.

Does every shipment into Egypt require the same ACI documents?

No. The relevant pre-arrival or pre-shipment filing and supporting documents depend on the transport mode, cargo and current regulations. Check the official Nafeza guidance and confirm applicability for your specific shipment before dispatch.

Can I request support for an Egyptian port other than Port Said?

Yes. We welcome enquiries involving ports throughout Egypt. Availability, routing and any local formalities will be checked for the requested port and cargo.
